Female human base mesh

This mesh is a vertex-level anatomical derivative of male-base.glb, created for
the FormForge character visualizer. It preserves the original topology while
using a distinct female silhouette with a narrower shoulder and waist frame,
wider pelvis and thighs, and separately modeled chest and glute contours.
